Rules of the road

One two-minute tea break at any of the three rest stops is compulsory. After three violations a police car stops you and fines one of them: Rs 1,000 for a red light, Rs 2,000 for overtaking across double lines. You then have 30 seconds to stop. You have three crash recoveries each journey.

Controls

W A S D or arrows: drive   Space: handbrake   H: horn   J: musical horn   L: lights
C: camera (chase, driver's seat, showcase)   I: journey info   M: map   R: reset bus
On a phone, use the buttons on the screen.

Ways to play

Play now needs no account; a guest's record is not kept. Sign up (top right) to keep your money and journeys, join daily challenges and post in the forum. Play with friends puts up to five drivers on the same road.

Fares are simulation fares, not official ones.
